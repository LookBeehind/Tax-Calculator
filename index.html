How it works

Each sale closes against the oldest unsold buy lot of the same security under FIFO. Lots persist across calendar years.

Both legs of every trade are converted to MDL using the BNM official rate on the trade date — so the tax base in MDL is independent of your USD result.

Commissions are added to cost on buys and subtracted from proceeds on sells before computing net profit.

Stock splits and reverse splits adjust quantity and per-share basis while preserving total cost. RSU vesting events add lots at vesting-date FMV.

Caveats

  • This is a calculation aid, not professional tax advice. Verify with fisc.md or a Moldovan tax advisor before filing.
  • Rates used: 6% on net capital gains (effective rate from 12% PIT × 50% taxable portion per Art. 40 al. 7), 12% on gross dividend income.
  • Loss years yield zero tax — no offset against dividend income, no carryforward to future years for individuals.
  • Foreign withholding tax shown but not applied as credit. Discuss with your accountant whether to claim it.

How to get your reports

From the IBKR Client Portal, generate an Annual Activity Statement and download it as CSV — one file per year you held positions.

1 Open Performance & Reports → Statements.
2 Open Activity Statement.
3 Set Period to Annual, pick the year, and click Download CSV. Repeat for each year you want to include.

Grab the official daily USD→MDL rates from bnm.md. Cover every year your IBKR statements span — earlier years too, in case the FIFO matcher reaches back to a buy lot before your earliest activity year.

1 Scroll to the bottom of the page and click Baza de date interactivă.
2 Uncheck every currency except USD (Dolar S.U.A.).
3 Set the date range to cover every activity year, choose XLSX, and click Generare.
